Welcome to Val Verde

Val Verde Flea Market, located in Donna, Texas, is a bustling marketplace offering a diverse array of goods and services to the community. Open every day of the week, it provides a unique shopping experience with a wide selection of vendors.

Visitors can explore various items, enjoy local culture, and savor great food, making it a vibrant destination for both locals and tourists.
